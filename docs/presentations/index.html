The Problem

Investors struggle to connect breaking news with portfolio impact

  • 📰 Breaking news about Apple → How much does it affect my position?
  • 💰 Did the price move match what the news predicted?
  • ⏰ Most investors discover the connection too late
  • 🔗 No system connects sentiment, price action, and position size
Traditional approach: Read news → Manually check price → Calculate impact (Hours too late)

Our Solution: FNPIS

📰 Aggregate News

Automatically pull financial news for every stock in your portfolio via Finnhub API

🤖 Sentiment Analysis

Use LLM Agent to classify each headline: Positive / Negative / Neutral

📊 Correlate with Price

Compare news sentiment with day's actual price movement

💹 Calculate Impact

Show exactly which holdings are affected and by how much

Demo Walkthrough

Scenario: User holds AAPL, NVDA, TSLA

July 28, 2026 morning

  • Step 1: System polls Finnhub → Fetches latest news for AAPL, NVDA, TSLA
  • Step 2: LLM analyzes: "Nvidia beats Q2 estimates" → POSITIVE sentiment
  • Step 3: Day closes → NVDA closes +3.2% → CONFIRMED (sentiment matched price)
  • Step 4: User sees: "News gained you $1,600 on your NVDA position"

Key Terms

Portfolio Your investment account (e.g., "My US Stock Account")
Holding One stock position (e.g., 10 shares of AAPL @ $180)
Security The stock itself (e.g., AAPL = Apple Inc.)
Sentiment News classification: POSITIVE / NEGATIVE / NEUTRAL
Alignment Did price agree with sentiment? CONFIRMED / DIVERGENT / INCONCLUSIVE
Impact Dollar impact on your position (what you gained/lost)

System Architecture

Three Data Flows

  • Portfolio & Holdings: Stored in database, user-controlled
  • Financial News: From Finnhub, polled every 15 minutes
  • Stock Prices: From Finnhub, fetched every 1 minute (market hours)

Scheduled Jobs (Dependency Order)

  1. News poll (15 min) → Sentiment analysis → Quote refresh (1 min)
  2. Daily closing snapshot (after market close) → Impact recompute

Two Questions, Never Blended

Direction What should this news have done? From Sentiment
Alignment Did the price actually agree? From Price Action
Why this matters:
DIVERGENT news (negative headline the market shrugged off) is the most valuable insight — a system that hides it is just confirming what investors already assumed.

How We Measure Success

  • Demo Correctness (SC-001): System must correctly classify sentiment and compute impact for 3+ worked examples
  • Arithmetic Accuracy (SC-002): Instructors manually verify portfolio value matches impact assessments
  • Data Freshness (SC-003): Quote updates within 1 min of market quote; news within 15 min
  • Edge Case Handling: All 24 edge cases (EC-01 to EC-24) covered by tests, no silent failures
  • Resilience: When Finnhub is down, read endpoints still work with stale data + stale: true flag
